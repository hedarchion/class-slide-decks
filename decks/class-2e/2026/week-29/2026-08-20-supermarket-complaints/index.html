WARM-UP · 4 MINUTES

What problem can you see?

Say a full sentence: “The biscuits are broken.”
✓ A complaint begins with a clear problem. What happened to the biscuits?
WARM-UP · SAY IT

Which one is a supermarket problem?

Tell your partner: “The milk is sour.”
✓ “Sour” means the milk has gone bad. It is a clear reason to complain.
BRIDGE · WHY NOT SHOUT?

Which customer sounds polite?

✓ The second customer starts politely and explains the reason. A complaint is firm, not rude.
YOUR FIRST PHRASE

Start a complaint politely.

“Excuse me. I’d like to make a complaint about this ___.”
✓ “I’d like to…” is a polite way to begin. It tells customer service what you need to do.
STATE THE PROBLEM

Add one clear reason.

Customer says:

“It is damaged.”
“It has expired.”
“It is the wrong item.”

Try it:

“I’d like to make a complaint about this cereal. It has expired.”

Point to the product as you speak.

ASK FOR A SOLUTION

Choose the polite request.

✓ “Could I have…?” asks politely. Add “please” at the end.
Say it together: “Could I have a refund, please?”
CUSTOMER SERVICE

How can customer service respond?

Use these phrases:

  • “I’m sorry about that.”
  • “Could I see the receipt, please?”
  • “Would you like a refund or an exchange?”

Listen for:

a polite apology

a question

a solution

  • “Could I have an exchange, please?”

Customer service: you must say

  • “I’m sorry about that.”
  • “Could I see the receipt?”
  • Offer an exchange.
