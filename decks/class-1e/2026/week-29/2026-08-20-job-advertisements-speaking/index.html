Which job fits you?

Read two job advertisements. Choose, compare and recommend.

I can explain my choice with clear reasons.
RETRIEVAL · USE A COMPLETE IDEA

Which answer gives a clear reason?

✓ C gives a choice and a complete reason after because.

READ · FIND THE IMPORTANT DETAILS

Two jobs, two different people

📚 Library Assistant

Sunshine Library

  • Organise and shelve books
  • Help students find books
  • Keep the study area quiet and clean

You need: to be friendly, helpful and free from 4–6 p.m.

🐕 Pet Sitter

Marigold Pet Care

  • Walk and play with pets
  • Clean up after them
  • Give medication if needed

You need: to love animals and enjoy being active outdoors.

SCAN · WHICH ADVERTISEMENT?

Find the evidence, then choose.

The library job states 4–6 p.m.; the pet-sitter advert mentions being active outdoors.

INFER · MATCH SKILLS TO A JOB

Which job suits Aina best?

Aina loves reading. She is patient and organised, but she cannot work outdoors.

✓ Library assistant. Her interests and strengths match the requirements; the outdoor job does not.

MODEL · CHOICE + REASON

Build a strong first answer.

I would like to be a _____ because I _____.library assistant · love books and reading
I would choose the _____ job because I am _____.pet sitter · active and caring
Choose one frame. Say it without reading every word.
COMPARE · ONE PLUS AND ONE MINUS

A good choice can still have a disadvantage.

“One advantage is _____. However, one disadvantage is _____.”
ERROR ANALYSIS · REPAIR THE ANSWER

What is missing?

“The advantage is flexible hours. However, difficult.”

✓ Repair: “The hours are flexible. However, caring for a sick pet can be difficult.”

RECOMMEND · DECIDE WHO IT SUITS

Do not say only “yes” or “no”.

Yes, I would recommend this job to people who _____.love animals / enjoy reading
No, I would not recommend it to someone who _____.dislikes outdoor work / cannot work from 4–6 p.m.
Give a recommendation and one reason.
PAIR REHEARSAL · PREPARE FOR THE BOOK

Ask, answer, then ask one follow-up.

1Which job would you like? Why?
2What is one advantage and one disadvantage?
3Would you recommend it? Why?
Follow-up: “Can you explain more?”
