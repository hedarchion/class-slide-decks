# Class 2D - FANBOYS Worksheet Answer Key

## Part A - 10 marks

1. A - **and**
2. B - **but**
3. B - **or**
4. A - **so**
5. C - **yet**
6. B - **for**
7. A - **nor**
8. B - **and**
9. B - **but**
10. C - **so**

Award 1 mark for each correct answer.

## Part B - 5 marks

11. Nadia collected cans, **and** Farid collected bottles.
12. The work was difficult, **but** the students did not give up.
13. Our team can clean the garden, **or** your team can decorate the hall.
14. The floor was wet, **so** we put up a warning sign.
15. The class did not leave rubbish, **nor did it forget** any tools.

Award 1 mark when the conjunction, clause grammar, and sentence punctuation are correct. Ignore minor handwriting issues that do not change the sentence.

## Part C - Email rubric - 10 marks

| Criterion | Marks | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Answers both explicit questions | 4 | 2 marks for a clear answer about joining; 2 marks for an activity and a reason. Award 1 mark for a partly developed answer in either area. |
| FANBOYS use | 3 | 1 mark each for up to three different, correctly used FANBOYS conjunctions. Do not award a mark for a conjunction that creates an unclear or ungrammatical link. |
| Email organisation | 2 | 1 mark for a suitable greeting/opening; 1 mark for a logical message and suitable closing. |
| A2 language control | 1 | Meaning is clear despite minor grammar, spelling, or punctuation errors. |

### Model response

**Subject: School Green Day**

Hi Aina,

Yes, I can join the School Green Day, and I am excited to help. I would like to collect cans and bottles because recycling keeps our school clean. I can work in the garden, but I do not have gardening gloves. I can bring rubbish bags, or I can make simple recycling signs at home. I can also ask my brother to lend me gloves. The work may be tiring, yet it will be useful for everyone. Please tell me what time we should meet.

See you,
Sara

The model is one acceptable answer, not a script learners must copy. Accept other relevant activities and reasons. The email should be 80-100 words; allow a small tolerance when all required evidence is present.

## Formative interpretation

- **13-15 grammar marks:** ready to apply FANBOYS in short writing.
- **9-12 grammar marks:** reteach the meanings or punctuation shown by errors, then retry two parallel sentences.
- **0-8 grammar marks:** model clause relationships with `and`, `but`, `or`, and `so` before revisiting `for`, `nor`, and `yet`.

This is a new, unpiloted classroom worksheet. It is designed for consistent formative marking, but it does not establish statistical reliability or full validity.
