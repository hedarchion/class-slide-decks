LIMITATION · WHY ITERATION CAN FAIL
AI has a context window.

It can work with only a limited amount of conversation, instructions and code at one time.

WHEN THE WINDOW GETS FULL

A very long chat and a large index.html compete for space.

Older details may not be included when the AI replies.

WHY YOUR PROJECT IS HEAVY

Three.js scenes, physics code, 3D assets and repeated versions use many tokens.

The AI may then repeat a mistake or change the wrong part.

This is an AI limitation—not proof that your team explained badly.
STRATEGY · FOCUSED ITERATION
When context gets crowded, make a fresh, focused request.

1. SAVE A CHECKPOINT

Keep a working copy before a big change.

2. SEND THE RELEVANT PART

Share the exact function, error and goal—not every file.

3. START WITH A BRIEF

In a new chat, state the game, the rules and what must not change.

“Our code was large, so we used focused prompts and relevant sections instead of asking AI to rewrite everything.”
