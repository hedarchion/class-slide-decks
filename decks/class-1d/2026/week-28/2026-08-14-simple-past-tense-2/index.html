BRIDGE · NOTICE

One story can have three shapes

StatementI played football.
NegativeI didn’t play football.
QuestionDid you play football?
What happens to played after did / didn’t?
It returns to the base verb: play. The helper did already shows past time.
MODEL · NEGATIVE

subject + didn’t + base verb
