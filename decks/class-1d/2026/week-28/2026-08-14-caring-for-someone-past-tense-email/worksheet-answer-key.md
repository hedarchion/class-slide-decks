# Class 1D - Caring for Someone: Answer Key and Rubric

## Page 1 - Past-tense scaffold - 10 marks

### Part A - 5 marks

1. B - **had**
2. B - **gave**
3. A - **made**
4. B - **took**
5. C - **felt**

Award 1 mark for each correct answer.

### Part B - 5 marks

6. **Last Sunday, Aina had a fever.**
7. **First, I checked her temperature.**
8. **Then, I gave her water and made soup.**  
   Award 1 mark for **gave** and 1 mark for **made** when each verb is used correctly in a complete sentence.
9. **Finally, she felt better.**

Ignore minor comma or spelling errors when the past-tense sentence remains clear. Do not accept present-tense target verbs for the grammar mark.

Part C is an unmarked planning scaffold. Check that each learner has enough notes to answer both email questions before moving to page 2.

## Page 2 - Email rubric - 10 marks

| Criterion | Marks | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Answers both questions | 4 | 2 marks for identifying the person and problem; 2 marks for describing helping actions and the person's final feeling. Award 1 mark for a partly developed response in either area. |
| Simple-past control | 3 | 3 marks: at least five appropriate past forms are mostly correct; 2 marks: three or four correct past forms; 1 mark: one or two correct past forms. |
| Organisation and sequencing | 2 | 1 mark for a suitable email opening/closing; 1 mark for at least two clear story-order words used logically. |
| A2 language clarity | 1 | Meaning is clear despite minor grammar, spelling, or punctuation errors. |

### Model response

**Subject: A time I helped someone**

Hi Aina,

Last Saturday, I took care of my younger brother. He had a fever and felt very tired. First, I checked his temperature and gave him some water. Then, I made warm soup, but he did not eat much. After that, I called my mother and stayed with him while he rested. I also gave him his medicine. We watched a quiet cartoon together before he slept. Finally, his temperature went down, and he felt better. I was tired, but I was happy that I could help him.

Take care,
Sara

The model is one acceptable response, not a script learners must copy. Accept a real or imagined experience involving a family member, friend, neighbour, or other person. Do not require learners to disclose private medical or family information.

## Formative interpretation

- **8-10 page 1 marks:** learner is ready to draft independently using the plan.
- **5-7 page 1 marks:** quickly reteach the incorrect irregular forms, then orally rehearse the four planned details.
- **0-4 page 1 marks:** use the toolbox to build two model sentences together before the learner writes the email.

This is a new, unpiloted classroom worksheet. It is designed for consistent formative marking but does not establish statistical reliability or full validity.
