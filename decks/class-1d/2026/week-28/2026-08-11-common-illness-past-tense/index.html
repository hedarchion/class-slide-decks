LOOK AT TIME

English changes when time changes

We can talk about now, a usual action, or yesterday.

YESTERDAY
I had a cough.
NOW
I have a cough.
RIGHT NOW
I am coughing.
Simple pastfinished: had / took / drank
Simple presentusual / now: have / take / drink
Present continuoushappening: am / is / are + -ing
